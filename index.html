Sandbox Conquest

TABS meets Age of Conquest IV — a 2D top-down territory conquest sandbox where you drop armies, watch chaotic battles, and paint the map your color.

Core Fantasy

You are a warlord (or god) shaping a continent. Drop armies onto a map, watch them clash with emergent, slightly-chaotic combat, and conquer territory. No rigid campaign — just a sandbox where you set up scenarios, tweak factions, and watch the world burn.

Map

Conquer Territory

Factions

Four distinct playstyles. Each has a color, flag, and 3–5 unit types.

Defense

Iron Legion

Heavy infantry, discipline. Holds the line; slow but unbreakable.

  • Swordsmen, pikemen, shield wall
  • Stronghold defense bonus
  • Weakness: low mobility
Speed

Steppe Horde

Cavalry raiders. Fast flanking and pursuit; fragile in prolonged melee.

  • Light riders, lancers
  • Raid economy: loot on victory
  • Weakness: poor vs. armor
Magic

Arcane Covenant

Magic & ranged. Area damage and morale shock; needs protection.

  • Archers, mages, slingers
  • Friendly-fire possible
  • Weakness: low HP
Swarm

Wildfolk

Beasts and swarm. Numbers and terrain bonuses (forest).

  • Beasts, skirmishers
  • Forest terrain bonus
  • Weakness: disorganized

The Map

~60–100 provinces. Each has an owner, terrain, resource yield, and fortification level.

Terrain Types

  • Plains — open, cavalry favored
  • Forest — Wildfolk bonus
  • Mountains — defense bonus
  • River — movement barrier
  • City — high yield, high fortification

Economy

  • Gold from provinces + tribute
  • Manpower pool regenerates slowly
  • Upkeep: unpaid armies desert
  • Recruit, upgrade, hire mercs

Combat — The TABS Flavor

Battles run in a small arena generated from the province terrain.

Default

Auto-Resolve

Weighted outcome from stats, terrain, morale, and random variance. Fast and decisive.

Watch

Real-Time Sim

Spawn units, run lightweight sim: move, attack, rout. Slightly chaotic — friendly fire, trampling.

Morale System

Units rout when HP or allies drop below threshold. Routed units flee; if caught, destroyed. Player influences pre-battle placement, terrain choice, and aggressiveness.

Enemy AI

Simple priority AI — expand into weak neighbors, defend threatened borders, ally against the leader. Difficulty = aggression + economic bonus. Basic diplomacy, no deep trees in v1.

Open Questions

Decisions still to make.

Hex vs. square provinces? Hex feels more strategic; square is simpler to render.
Turn-based map + real-time battles (recommended) vs. fully real-time.
How "TABS" should battles feel? Ragdoll physics is hard in 2D; propose emergent unit AI instead.
Multiplayer? Deferred to v2+.

Out of Scope (v1)

3D, modding API, multiplayer, deep diplomacy trees, naval combat.
